Electronics Technician

Electronics Technicians assemble, install, maintain, and repair electronic equipment and systems. They work with circuit boards, sensors, microcontrollers, and various electronic devices, ensuring proper functioning and troubleshooting faults in industrial, commercial, and consumer electronics.

Relevant Subjects

Basic Electronics Analog & Digital Circuits Electronic Devices Microcontrollers Instrumentation PCB Design Electrical Machines Troubleshooting Techniques

Required Skills

Soldering & Assembly Circuit Testing Equipment Calibration Reading Schematics Fault Diagnosis Use of Multimeter & Oscilloscope Safety Procedures Documentation

Career Path

Trainee Technician
Junior Electronics Technician
Electronics Technician
Senior Technician
Supervisor/Team Lead
Service Manager

Salary Range

₹1,80,000 - ₹4,50,000 per annum (depending on experience, skills, and organization)

Industry Demand

Electronics Technicians are in demand in manufacturing, telecommunications, consumer electronics, automotive, healthcare equipment, and industrial automation sectors. Their expertise is essential for maintaining and repairing critical electronic systems.

Work Environment

Electronics Technicians work in labs, workshops, factories, or on-site at client locations. The role may involve shift work, field visits, and hands-on tasks, often as part of a technical team.

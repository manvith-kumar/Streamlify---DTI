Structural Drafter

Structural Drafters create detailed drawings and plans for buildings, bridges, and other structures based on engineering specifications. They use CAD software to prepare layouts, sections, and details, ensuring accuracy and compliance with standards. Their work supports engineers and architects in the design and construction process.

Relevant Subjects

Engineering Drawing Structural Mechanics Construction Materials CAD (AutoCAD, Revit) Building Codes & Standards Mathematics Surveying Basic Civil Engineering

Required Skills

AutoCAD/Revit Technical Drawing Attention to Detail Understanding Blueprints Communication Teamwork Problem Solving Time Management

Career Path

Structural Drafter

Salary Range

₹2,00,000 - ₹6,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Structural Drafters are in demand in construction, infrastructure, real estate, and engineering consulting firms. Their expertise is essential for translating engineering concepts into actionable plans for safe and efficient construction.

Work Environment

Structural Drafters typically work in offices, collaborating with engineers, architects, and project managers. The role involves working with CAD software, reviewing designs, and occasionally visiting construction sites for coordination.

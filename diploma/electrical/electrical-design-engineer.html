Electrical Design Engineer

Electrical Design Engineers create, develop, and test electrical systems and components for a wide range of projects, including power distribution, control systems, and electronic devices. They use specialized software to design schematics, ensure compliance with safety standards, and collaborate with multidisciplinary teams to deliver efficient and reliable solutions.

Relevant Subjects

Electrical Machines Circuit Theory Power Systems Control Systems Electrical Drawing Electronics CAD for Electrical Measurement & Instrumentation

Required Skills

AutoCAD/E-Plan Electrical Schematic Design Load Calculations Project Management Problem Solving Knowledge of Standards (IEC/IS) Teamwork Communication

Career Path

Salary Range

₹2,50,000 - ₹8,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Electrical Design Engineers are in demand in industries such as construction, manufacturing, power generation, automation, and infrastructure. Their expertise is essential for designing safe, efficient, and innovative electrical systems in buildings, factories, and public utilities.

Work Environment

Electrical Design Engineers typically work in offices, design labs, or on-site at project locations. They collaborate with architects, civil engineers, and project managers, and may visit sites to oversee installation and commissioning of electrical systems.

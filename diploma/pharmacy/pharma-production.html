Pharma Production Executive

Pharma Production Executives oversee the manufacturing of pharmaceutical products, ensuring compliance with quality standards, safety protocols, and regulatory requirements. They coordinate production schedules, supervise staff, monitor equipment, and maintain documentation for audits and inspections.

Relevant Subjects

Pharmaceutics Pharmaceutical Chemistry Pharmaceutical Engineering Quality Assurance Microbiology Industrial Pharmacy Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP)

Required Skills

Production Planning Process Optimization Documentation & Record Keeping Team Supervision Quality Control Regulatory Compliance Problem Solving Communication

Career Path

Production Trainee
Production Officer
Production Executive
Production Manager
Plant Head
Operations Director

Salary Range

₹2,00,000 - ₹8,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Pharma Production Executives are in demand in pharmaceutical manufacturing companies, contract manufacturing organizations, and biotech firms. Their role is vital for ensuring uninterrupted production, product quality, and regulatory compliance.

Work Environment

Pharma Production Executives work in manufacturing plants, often in cleanroom environments. The job involves shift work, team coordination, and adherence to strict safety and quality protocols.

Maintenance Engineer

Maintenance Engineers are responsible for ensuring the smooth operation and reliability of machinery, equipment, and systems in manufacturing plants and industrial facilities. They plan and execute preventive and corrective maintenance, troubleshoot breakdowns, and work to improve equipment efficiency and safety.

Relevant Subjects

Mechanical Engineering Industrial Maintenance Thermodynamics Machine Design Hydraulics & Pneumatics Electrical Basics Manufacturing Processes Safety & Quality Management

Required Skills

Troubleshooting Preventive Maintenance Root Cause Analysis Technical Documentation Teamwork Communication Safety Awareness Time Management

Career Path

Maintenance Technician
Junior Maintenance Engineer
Maintenance Engineer
Senior Maintenance Engineer
Maintenance Manager
Plant Head/Operations Manager

Salary Range

₹2,00,000 - ₹6,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Maintenance Engineers are in demand in sectors such as manufacturing, automotive, power plants, oil & gas, and process industries. Their expertise is vital for minimizing downtime, optimizing equipment performance, and ensuring workplace safety.

Work Environment

Maintenance Engineers work in industrial settings, often on the shop floor or in plants. The role involves hands-on work, shift duties, coordination with production teams, and sometimes responding to emergencies or breakdowns.

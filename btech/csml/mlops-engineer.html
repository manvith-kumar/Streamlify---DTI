MLOps Engineer

MLOps Engineers bridge the gap between data science and IT operations. They design, build, and maintain the infrastructure and tools needed to deploy, monitor, and scale machine learning models in production. Their work ensures that ML models are robust, reproducible, and efficiently integrated into business applications.

Relevant Subjects

Machine Learning DevOps Cloud Computing Software Engineering Data Engineering Programming (Python/Java) Containerization (Docker/Kubernetes) CI/CD Pipelines

Required Skills

Model Deployment Automation Cloud Platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP) Containerization CI/CD Monitoring & Logging Programming (Python/Shell) Collaboration

Career Path

DevOps/Software Engineer
Junior MLOps Engineer
MLOps Engineer
Senior MLOps Engineer
MLOps Architect
Head of MLOps/Engineering

Salary Range

₹8,00,000 - ₹40,00,000 per annum (based on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

MLOps Engineers are in high demand across industries adopting AI and ML at scale, including technology, finance, healthcare, e-commerce, and manufacturing. Their expertise is crucial for operationalizing ML solutions and ensuring their reliability in production.

Work Environment

MLOps Engineers typically work in collaborative, fast-paced environments, often as part of cross-functional teams with data scientists, software engineers, and IT professionals. The role involves continuous learning and adapting to evolving tools and best practices in ML and DevOps.

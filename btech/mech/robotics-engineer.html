Robotics Engineer

Robotics Engineers design, build, and maintain robots and automated systems that can perform tasks in industries such as manufacturing, healthcare, automotive, and aerospace. They integrate mechanical, electrical, and software engineering to create intelligent machines that improve efficiency, safety, and productivity.

Relevant Subjects

Mechanics Control Systems Mechatronics Electronics Programming (C/C++/Python) Sensors & Actuators Artificial Intelligence Robotics Design

Required Skills

Mechanical Design Embedded Systems Programming (C/C++/Python) CAD Modeling Problem Solving Teamwork System Integration Project Management

Career Path

Robotics Intern
Junior Robotics Engineer
Robotics Engineer
Senior Robotics Engineer
Lead Robotics Engineer
Robotics Project Manager

Salary Range

₹5,00,000 - ₹25,00,000 per annum (based on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Robotics Engineers are in high demand across manufacturing, automotive, healthcare, logistics, and research sectors. The push for automation and Industry 4.0 is driving rapid growth in robotics applications and career opportunities.

Work Environment

Robotics Engineers work in labs, manufacturing plants, or offices, often collaborating with multidisciplinary teams. The role may involve hands-on prototyping, simulation, testing, and field deployment of robotic systems.

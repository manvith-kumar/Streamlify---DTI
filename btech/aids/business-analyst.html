Business Analyst

Business Analysts bridge the gap between business objectives and technology solutions. They analyze data, processes, and systems to provide actionable insights, recommend improvements, and support decision-making. Business Analysts play a crucial role in driving organizational efficiency, digital transformation, and strategic planning.

Relevant Subjects

Business Analytics Statistics Data Mining Database Management Operations Research Management Information Systems Programming (Python/SQL) Economics

Required Skills

Data Analysis Requirement Gathering Process Modeling Communication Problem Solving SQL & Excel Visualization Tools (Power BI/Tableau) Stakeholder Management

Career Path

Business Analyst Intern
Junior Business Analyst
Business Analyst
Senior Business Analyst
Lead/Principal Analyst

Director of Business Analysis

Salary Range

₹5,00,000 - ₹18,00,000 per annum (based on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Business Analysts are in high demand across industries such as IT, finance, consulting, healthcare, retail, and manufacturing. Their ability to interpret data and align business goals with technology makes them valuable assets in digital transformation and data-driven decision-making.

Work Environment

Business Analysts typically work in collaborative office environments, interacting with stakeholders, management, and technical teams. The role may involve meetings, workshops, data analysis, and documentation.

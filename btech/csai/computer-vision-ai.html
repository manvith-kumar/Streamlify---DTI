Computer Vision AI Engineer

Computer Vision AI Engineers develop algorithms and models that enable computers to interpret and process visual information from the world. They work on tasks such as image classification, object detection, facial recognition, and scene understanding, applying deep learning and machine learning techniques to solve real-world problems in industries like healthcare, automotive, security, and entertainment.

Relevant Subjects

Computer Vision Image Processing Deep Learning Machine Learning Mathematics (Linear Algebra, Calculus) Probability & Statistics Programming (Python/C++) Pattern Recognition

Required Skills

Image & Video Analysis Deep Learning Frameworks (TensorFlow/PyTorch) OpenCV Algorithm Development Data Annotation & Augmentation Programming (Python/C++/MATLAB) Problem Solving Collaboration

Career Path

Computer Vision Intern
Junior Computer Vision Engineer
Computer Vision AI Engineer
Senior Computer Vision Engineer
Lead AI Scientist (Computer Vision)
Director of Computer Vision

Salary Range

₹10,00,000 - ₹50,00,000 per annum (based on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Computer Vision AI Engineers are in high demand across industries such as healthcare (medical imaging), automotive (autonomous vehicles), security (surveillance), retail (visual search), and entertainment (AR/VR). Their expertise is essential for building intelligent systems that can "see" and understand the world.

Work Environment

They typically work in collaborative, research-driven environments, often as part of interdisciplinary teams. The role involves experimenting with new models, publishing research, and staying updated with the latest advancements in computer vision and AI.

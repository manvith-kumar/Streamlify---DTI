Data Analyst (AI)

Data Analysts (AI) leverage artificial intelligence and machine learning tools to extract insights from large datasets. They clean, process, and analyze data, build predictive models, and visualize results to support business decisions. Their work bridges the gap between raw data and actionable intelligence, often collaborating with data scientists and engineers.

Relevant Subjects

Statistics Probability Data Mining Machine Learning Database Management Programming (Python/R/SQL) Data Visualization Business Intelligence

Required Skills

Data Cleaning Statistical Analysis Machine Learning Basics Data Visualization (Tableau/Power BI/Matplotlib) SQL & Database Querying Programming (Python/R) Critical Thinking Communication

Career Path

Data Analyst Intern
Junior Data Analyst
Data Analyst
Senior Data Analyst
Lead Data Analyst
Data Science Manager

Salary Range

₹5,00,000 - ₹18,00,000 per annum (based on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Data Analysts with AI skills are in high demand across industries such as finance, healthcare, e-commerce, consulting, and technology. Organizations rely on them to make data-driven decisions and gain a competitive edge.

Work Environment

Data Analysts (AI) typically work in collaborative office environments, often as part of analytics or business intelligence teams. The role may involve working with cross-functional teams and presenting findings to stakeholders.

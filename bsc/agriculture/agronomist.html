Agronomist

Agronomists are agricultural scientists who specialize in crop production and soil management. They work to improve the quality and yield of crops by researching and implementing best practices in planting, fertilization, irrigation, pest control, and sustainable farming techniques.

Relevant Subjects

Crop Production Soil Science Plant Physiology Agricultural Chemistry Genetics & Plant Breeding Irrigation & Water Management Pest Management Agricultural Economics

Required Skills

Field Research Soil & Crop Analysis Data Interpretation Problem Solving Communication Report Writing Teamwork Technology Adaptation

Career Path

Agronomy Intern
Junior Agronomist
Agronomist
Senior Agronomist
Agronomy Manager
Head of Agronomy/Research

Salary Range

₹3,00,000 - ₹10,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Agronomists are in demand in sectors such as agribusiness, research institutions, government agencies, seed and fertilizer companies, and NGOs. Their expertise is vital for ensuring food security, sustainable agriculture, and adapting to climate change.

Work Environment

Agronomists work both in the field and in laboratories, collaborating with farmers, researchers, and agricultural companies. The role involves field visits, data collection, experimentation, and providing recommendations for crop improvement.

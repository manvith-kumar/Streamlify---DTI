Chemist

Chemists study the composition, properties, and reactions of substances to develop new products or processes. They work in laboratories to analyze materials, conduct experiments, and ensure quality control in industries such as pharmaceuticals, food, environment, and manufacturing.

Relevant Subjects

Organic Chemistry Inorganic Chemistry Physical Chemistry Analytical Chemistry Mathematics Physics Environmental Science Laboratory Techniques

Required Skills

Analytical Skills Laboratory Skills Attention to Detail Problem Solving Data Analysis Report Writing Teamwork Safety Procedures

Salary Range

₹2,50,000 - ₹8,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Chemists are in demand in sectors like pharmaceuticals, chemicals, food & beverages, environmental agencies, and research organizations. Their expertise is vital for product development, quality assurance, and regulatory compliance.

Work Environment

Chemists typically work in laboratories, research centers, or industrial plants. The role involves handling chemicals, operating instruments, documenting results, and collaborating with scientists and engineers.

Biotech Analyst

Biotech Analysts apply scientific and analytical skills to evaluate biological data, conduct experiments, and support research in biotechnology. They work in areas such as pharmaceuticals, agriculture, food technology, and environmental science, helping to develop new products, improve processes, and ensure quality control.

Relevant Subjects

Microbiology Biochemistry Genetics Molecular Biology Biostatistics Bioinformatics Analytical Techniques Cell Biology

Required Skills

Laboratory Techniques Data Analysis Report Writing Critical Thinking Quality Control Research Methodology Communication Teamwork

Career Path

Biotech Analyst

Salary Range

₹3,00,000 - ₹10,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Biotech Analysts are in demand in pharmaceutical companies, research labs, food and beverage industries, agriculture, and environmental organizations. Their expertise is vital for innovation, regulatory compliance, and advancing scientific knowledge.

Work Environment

Biotech Analysts typically work in laboratories, research facilities, or corporate offices. The role involves hands-on experiments, data interpretation, collaboration with scientists, and adherence to safety and quality standards.

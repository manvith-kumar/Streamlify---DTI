Clinical Researcher

Clinical Researchers design, conduct, and analyze clinical trials and studies to evaluate the safety and effectiveness of medical treatments, drugs, and devices. They ensure compliance with regulatory standards, collect and interpret data, and contribute to advancements in healthcare and medicine.

Relevant Subjects

Microbiology Biochemistry Pharmacology Clinical Trials Biostatistics Research Methodology Ethics in Research Molecular Biology

Required Skills

Study Design Data Collection & Analysis Regulatory Compliance (GCP, ICH) Report Writing Attention to Detail Teamwork Critical Thinking Communication

Career Path

Clinical Research Intern
Clinical Research Associate
Clinical Researcher
Senior Clinical Researcher
Clinical Project Manager
Director of Clinical Research

Salary Range

₹3,00,000 - ₹12,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Clinical Researchers are in demand in pharmaceutical companies, hospitals, contract research organizations (CROs), and academic institutions. Their work is vital for the development of new therapies, vaccines, and medical devices, ensuring patient safety and regulatory approval.

Work Environment

Clinical Researchers work in multidisciplinary teams with doctors, scientists, statisticians, and regulatory professionals. The role involves both office-based tasks and on-site monitoring, requiring strong organizational and analytical skills.

Physical Design Engineer

Physical Design Engineers are responsible for transforming digital circuit designs into physical layouts for semiconductor fabrication. They work on floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, timing closure, and design rule checks to ensure that integrated circuits meet performance, power, and area requirements.

Relevant Subjects

VLSI Design Digital Electronics CMOS Technology Physical Design Automation Semiconductor Physics Computer Architecture EDA Tools Timing Analysis

Required Skills

Floorplanning Placement & Routing Static Timing Analysis (STA) Clock Tree Synthesis (CTS) Power Analysis Design Rule Checking (DRC) Scripting (Tcl/Perl/Python) Problem Solving Teamwork

Career Path

Physical Design Intern
Junior Physical Design Engineer
Physical Design Engineer
Senior Physical Design Engineer
Physical Design Lead
Physical Design Manager/Architect

Salary Range

₹6,00,000 - ₹35,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Physical Design Engineers are highly sought after in semiconductor, electronics, and EDA tool companies. Their expertise is crucial for developing advanced chips used in smartphones, computers, automotive, IoT, and communication devices.

Work Environment

Physical Design Engineers typically work in collaborative teams with design, verification, and fabrication engineers. The role involves using EDA tools, scripting, and close interaction with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality silicon products.

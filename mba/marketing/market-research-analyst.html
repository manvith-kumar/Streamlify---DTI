Market Research Analyst

Market Research Analysts collect and analyze data on consumers, competitors, and market conditions to help companies understand what products people want, who will buy them, and at what price. They use statistical techniques and software to interpret data, identify trends, and provide actionable insights for strategic decision-making.

Relevant Subjects

Marketing Management Consumer Behavior Statistics Market Research Business Analytics Data Analysis Economics Strategic Management

Required Skills

Data Collection & Analysis Survey Design Statistical Tools (SPSS, Excel, R) Report Writing Critical Thinking Presentation Skills Problem Solving Communication

Career Path

Market Research Intern
Junior Market Research Analyst
Market Research Analyst
Senior Market Research Analyst
Market Research Manager
Head of Market Insights

Salary Range

₹4,00,000 - ₹18,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Market Research Analysts are in demand across industries such as FMCG, retail, consulting, e-commerce, healthcare, and finance. Their insights are vital for launching new products, understanding market trends, and staying ahead of competitors.

Work Environment

Market Research Analysts typically work in office settings, collaborating with marketing, sales, and product teams. The role involves both independent data analysis and teamwork for project execution and client presentations.

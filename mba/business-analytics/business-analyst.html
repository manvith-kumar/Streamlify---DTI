Business Analyst

Business Analysts bridge the gap between business objectives and data-driven decision making. They analyze data, identify trends, and provide actionable insights to improve business processes, products, and strategies. Business Analysts work closely with stakeholders to understand requirements and translate them into analytical solutions.

Relevant Subjects

Business Analytics Statistics Data Visualization Operations Research Database Management Economics Finance Programming (Excel/Python/R)

Required Skills

Data Analysis SQL Data Visualization Tools (Tableau/Power BI) Requirement Gathering Problem Solving Communication Critical Thinking Stakeholder Management

Career Path

Business Analyst Intern
Junior Business Analyst
Business Analyst
Senior Business Analyst
Business Analytics Manager
Director/Head of Analytics

Salary Range

₹6,00,000 - ₹25,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Business Analysts are in demand across sectors such as consulting, finance, IT, healthcare, retail, and manufacturing. Their ability to turn data into actionable business insights is critical for organizations aiming to stay competitive and agile.

Work Environment

Business Analysts work in collaborative environments, interacting with business stakeholders, IT teams, and management. The role involves meetings, data analysis, report creation, and presenting findings to drive business decisions.

Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Our Commitment to Privacy

We respect your privacy. We do not store personal data unless submitted via the contact form. Your data is never shared with third parties.

Information We Collect

The only personal information we collect is what you voluntarily provide through our contact form, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Message content

How We Use Your Information

Any information collected through our contact form is used solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiries or feedback. We do not use this information for marketing purposes or share it with any third parties.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use cookies or any tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activities.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

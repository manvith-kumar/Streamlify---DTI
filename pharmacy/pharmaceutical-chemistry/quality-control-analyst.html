Quality Control Analyst – Pharmaceutical Chemistry

A Quality Control Analyst ensures pharmaceutical products meet required standards by conducting tests and inspections on raw materials, in-process samples, and finished goods. They play a crucial role in maintaining product safety, efficacy, and compliance with regulatory guidelines.

Relevant Subjects

Pharmaceutical Chemistry Pharmaceutical Analysis Physical Pharmacy Biochemistry Pharmacognosy Good Laboratory Practices (GLP) Quality Assurance

Required Skills

Analytical Techniques (HPLC, UV, IR) Documentation & SOPs Attention to Detail Regulatory Compliance Sampling and Testing Instrument Calibration Problem-Solving

Career Path

QC Intern
QC Analyst
Senior QC Analyst
QC Team Lead
Quality Control Manager
Director of Quality

Salary Range

₹3,00,000 - ₹10,00,000 per annum (based on experience and organization)

Industry Demand

The pharmaceutical industry's focus on high-quality production and compliance has created consistent demand for QC analysts. This role is essential in ensuring drug safety and regulatory approval across domestic and international markets.

Work Environment

QC Analysts typically work in laboratory settings within pharmaceutical manufacturing units. Their environment is highly regulated, requiring adherence to GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices), and involves routine sample testing, record-keeping, and equipment maintenance.

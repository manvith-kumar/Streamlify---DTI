Analytical Chemist

Analytical Chemists analyze chemical compounds to ensure drug safety and efficacy. They develop and validate analytical methods, perform tests on pharmaceutical substances and products, and interpret data to ensure compliance with regulatory standards. Their work is crucial in drug development, quality control, and stability testing throughout a product's lifecycle.

Relevant Subjects

Pharmaceutical Analysis Instrumental Methods Spectroscopy Chromatography Quality Assurance Method Validation Pharmacopoeial Standards Biopharmaceutics

Required Skills

HPLC/GC Operation Spectroscopic Techniques Method Development Data Interpretation GMP/GLP Compliance Problem Solving Technical Writing Instrument Maintenance

Industry Demand

Analytical Chemists are consistently in demand across pharmaceutical companies, contract research organizations, and regulatory bodies. With increasing regulatory requirements and the complexity of new drug formulations, there is a growing need for skilled analytical chemists who can develop robust methods and ensure product quality throughout the drug development lifecycle.

Work Environment

Analytical Chemists primarily work in laboratory settings equipped with sophisticated instrumentation. The role involves methodical testing procedures, careful documentation, and collaboration with formulation scientists, quality teams, and regulatory affairs professionals. They may work in research and development, quality control, or quality assurance departments of pharmaceutical companies or analytical testing laboratories.

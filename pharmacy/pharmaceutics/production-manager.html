Production Manager

Production Managers in pharmaceutical manufacturing oversee the efficient production of medicines while ensuring compliance with quality standards and regulations. They plan and coordinate production activities, manage staff, optimize processes, troubleshoot technical issues, and collaborate with quality assurance, maintenance, and supply chain departments.

Relevant Subjects

Industrial Pharmacy Pharmaceutical Technology GMP and Validation Quality Management Systems Pharmaceutics Supply Chain Management Process Engineering Production Planning

Required Skills

Production Planning Team Management GMP Compliance Process Optimization Problem-Solving Quality Control Resource Management Leadership

Career Path

Production Officer/Supervisor
Assistant Production Manager
Production Manager
Senior Production Manager
Production Head
Director of Manufacturing

Salary Range

₹6,00,000 - ₹28,00,000 per annum (depending on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Production Managers are in consistent demand across pharmaceutical manufacturing companies of all sizes. Their expertise is essential for maintaining efficient operations, ensuring product quality, and meeting production targets while complying with the strict regulatory requirements of the pharmaceutical industry.

Work Environment

Production Managers divide their time between production floor oversight and office-based planning and administration. The role involves direct interaction with production staff, equipment, and processes, as well as collaboration with quality assurance, regulatory, maintenance, and supply chain teams. The position may require shift work or extended hours to address production challenges.

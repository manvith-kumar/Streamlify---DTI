Pharmacovigilance Officer

Pharmacovigilance Officers monitor, assess, and ensure the safety of pharmaceutical products. They analyze adverse drug reactions (ADRs), maintain regulatory compliance, and contribute to improving drug safety worldwide.

Relevant Subjects

Pharmacology Drug Safety & Toxicology Pharmaceutical Sciences Clinical Trials & Research Healthcare Regulations Biostatistics Risk Management Quality Assurance

Required Skills

Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) Reporting Regulatory Compliance Clinical Data Analysis Risk Assessment & Management Medical Writing & Documentation Quality Assurance Problem Solving Collaboration

Career Path

Pharmacovigilance Officer

Salary Range

₹5,00,000 - ₹22,00,000 per annum (based on experience, expertise, and organization)

Industry Demand

Pharmacovigilance Officers are crucial in pharmaceutical companies, regulatory bodies, hospitals, and research organizations. Their work ensures drug safety and effectiveness, making them essential in healthcare and drug development industries.

Work Environment

Pharmacovigilance Officers work in pharmaceutical firms, hospitals, clinical research organizations, and regulatory agencies. Their role involves analyzing patient-reported drug effects, collaborating with healthcare teams, and ensuring compliance with global safety standards.
